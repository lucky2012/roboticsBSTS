Group 1 project

TOPIC- Smart City

Features

1. Smart irrigation system for garden
2. Smart traffic control with AI
3. Smart parking
4. Automatic fire sensor
5. Automatic water drainage or sewage management
6. Automatic waste management
7. Automatic street lights

Smart irrigation system

Uses a soil moisture sensor to water plants automatically in a garden or Park.

Smart parking

IR sensor detects car entering and exiting and anal CD shows the availability of parking slots with many other features.

Automatic fire sensor

Fire sensor in building detect fire and signals and Note 5 department etc we can use the dashboard to show it.

Automatic flood detection

Places which sit below high places (down city) often get flooded when it rains when there is excessive water we can use ultrasonic sensor or water level sensor to detect whether it is flooding or not. Then we can use a water pump to drain the water.

Automatic waste management

Places like market with her filled with trash can be filled with multiple dustbins which notification when they are full we can use an ultrasonic sensor to detect when the trash can is full.

Automatic street lights

Street lights (shown with LED's) automatically turn on when it becomes dark at night.
